# Lubaina Himid

Expanded for batch 2, October 2026. Facts added by the expansion:

- A Fashionable Marriage (1986): Hogarth's Marriage A-la-Mode figures cut out as a life-size tableau of 1980s London; Thatcher and Reagan as the countess and the lawyer Silvertongue: https://www.paul-mellon-centre.ac.uk/whats-on/past/from-hogarth-to-thatcher-lubaina-himids-a-fashionable-marriage-1986 ; https://edition.cnn.com/style/article/turner-prize-winner-2017-lubaina-himid ; https://www.festival-automne.com/en/edition-2026/lubaina-himid-a-fashionable-marriage
- Naming the Money (2004): 100 life-size cut-out figures given roles (ceramicists, herbalists, shoemakers) and a soundtrack; presented by the artist to the International Slavery Museum, National Museums Liverpool; 20 figures shown at the Walker Art Gallery; shown at the Royal Academy in Entangled Pasts, 1768-now (2024). One UCLan record dates the work to 2014; the page keeps 2004: https://royalacademy.org.uk/art-artists/work-of-art/naming-the-money-sketches ; https://elephant.art/lubaina-himid-naming-the-money-2004-14072020 ; https://corridor8.co.uk/article/lubaina-himid-meticulous-observations-naming-money/
- Swallow Hard: The Lancaster Dinner Service (2007), a repainted dinner set telling the story of slavery: https://edition.cnn.com/style/article/turner-prize-winner-2017-lubaina-himid
- Turner Prize 2017 exhibition at the Ferens Art Gallery, Hull: https://clok.uclan.ac.uk/22965 ; CBE in 2018: Tate press release below.
