Caleb Azumah Nelson was born in 1993 and grew up in south-east London, in a family of Ghanaian heritage. He studied at Coventry University and began making both photographs and writing, finding in each a way to record Black life in London with tenderness.

His debut novel, *Open Water* (2021), follows two young Black British artists, a photographer and a dancer, as they fall in and out of love; it is written in the second person and pays close attention to how moments look as well as how they feel. It won the Costa First Novel Award and the British Book Award for debut of the year. He has since published *Small Worlds* (2023). Before his first novel he had been shortlisted for the Palm Photo Prize.
