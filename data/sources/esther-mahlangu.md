# Esther Mahlangu

Expanded for batch 3, October 2026. Facts added or corrected by the expansion:

- Taught wall painting and beadwork at about ten by her mother and grandmother: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_Mahlangu (pointer) ; https://momaa.org/then-i-knew-i-was-good-at-painting/
- Freehand, no stencils or tape, chicken-feather brushes; early pigments natural, mixed with clay, soil and cow dung (the "How to look" paragraph is the site's own reading built on this): https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/about/press/serpentine-to-unveil-large-scale-mural-by-esther-mahlangu/ ; https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/14/03/2017/South-Africa-s-chicken-feather-painter-brushes-off-fame
- Magiciens de la Terre: French embassy researchers incl. André Magnin visited in 1986 and saw her house paintings; she painted a replica of her home: https://sahistory.org.za/people/esther-mahlangu ; https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/south-africa/article/detail/T0446392EN/
- BMW 525i, 1991, the twelfth Art Car; returned to South Africa; Iziko Museums, Cape Town, 18 Feb - 11 Aug 2024: https://news.uj.ac.za/news/renowned-artist-esther-mahlangus-bmw-art-car-returns-to-sa-after-32-years-to-grace-cape-towns-iziko-museum/ ; https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/south-africa/article/detail/T0439118EN/
- Brand collaborations: Belvedere (RED, against AIDS), Fiat, British Airways; BMW 7 Series at Frieze London: https://edition.cnn.com/style/article/esther-mahlangu . Removed: Rolls-Royce (single source).
- Teaches young people in her village (replaces "opened a school at her home", not confirmed): https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/14/03/2017/South-Africa-s-chicken-feather-painter-brushes-off-fame
- Honorary doctorate, University of Johannesburg, 2018; Order of Ikhamanga in silver: https://abc17news.com/news/2024/12/27/south-african-retrospective-exhibition-honors-the-colorful-work-of-artist-esther-mahlangu/ ; https://www.voazimbabwe.com/a/6586063.html
- Serpentine mural Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu, Oct 2024 - Mar 2026, first UK public artwork; Ross + Kramer, Time in Color, opened 21 Nov 2024: https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/about/press/serpentine-to-unveil-large-scale-mural-by-esther-mahlangu/ ; https://www.meer.com/en/86011-time-in-color
- Not used: Weltevrede birthplace; "first non-Western artist" wording.

Where to see the work: Iziko Museums (the car, in 2024; check current display); Serpentine mural ended March 2026; check galleries.
