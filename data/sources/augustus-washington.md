# Augustus Washington

Enriched for batch 8, October 2026 (kept short).

- Born 1820, Trenton; Dartmouth 1843 (no degree); Hartford studio 1846; Liberia 1853; Speaker of the House 1865 to 1869; died Monrovia 7 June 1875. The earlier "senator" was unverified and was removed from the page and the summary.
- John Brown daguerreotype: National Portrait Gallery bought it at auction in 1996; Nelson-Atkins holds a separate quarter-plate copy.
- Sources: Smithsonian magazine, Smarthistory, museum records.
