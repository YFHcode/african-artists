# Chéri Samba

Expanded for batch 5, October 2026.

- Left school in 1972 to apprentice with sign painters on Kasa-Vubu Avenue; borrowed the speech bubble from comics in 1975; own work from 1979; main subject of his paintings from the late 1980s: https://www.fondationcartier.com/en/collection/artists/cheri-samba ; https://momaa.org/icons-african-art/cheri-samba/ ; https://www.universalis.fr/encyclopedie/cheri-samba/
- Magiciens de la Terre, Pompidou, 1989, curator Jean-Hubert Martin; Fondation Cartier residency 1990; J'aime Chéri Samba 2004, thirty-five paintings, first solo show there: Fondation Cartier
- Quel avenir pour notre art ? (1997) triptych, Samba and Picasso carry paintings into the Pompidou: Universalis; https://museumgeographies.com/2014/08/21/exploring-othering-through-the-work-of-congolese-pop-artist-cheri-samba/ ; Musée Maillol retrospective 2023-24, first, forty years: https://movieintheair.com/en/2023/10/cheri-samba-artist/
- Collections: Pompidou, MoMA; Zeitz MOCAA lists him as an artist: https://zeitzmocaa.museum/artists/cheri-samba/
- "How to look" is the site's reading.
