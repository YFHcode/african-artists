# Competitor research — October 2026

Who ranks for the topics this site covers, what wins for them, and what to do about it.
Sources: 32 web searches on our topics (31 October 2026) and Semrush screenshots of
momaa.org (US database, 30 October 2026), shared by the owner during a Semrush trial.

## Who the competitors are

Semrush's own competitor list for africanartists.com (Smarthistory, umaine.edu, Etsy, YouTube,
Wikipedia…) shares one keyword with us each and is not useful. Searching our topics found these
instead.

**Same kind of site (artist profiles, art-form guides, lists):**

- **momaa.org**: an online gallery with a large journal: artist profiles ("Icons of African
  art"), art-form guides (bogolanfini, Benin Bronzes), a collecting guide and a directory of
  African museums. Appeared in 10 of 32 searches. The closest match to this site.
- **thecollector.com**: general art-history magazine (photographers, masks, Benin Bronzes,
  Marlene Dumas).
- **afrikanizm.com**: gallery blog (influential-artists lists, women artists, collecting, fairs).
- **artindexafrica.substack.com**: newsletter (contemporary art history, women artists, biennales).
- **artsandculture.google.com**: museum stories (Nigerian masters, photographers, masks).
- Marketplace blogs: blog.artsper.com (also first for the French list query), singulart,
  tingatingaart.com, artincontext.org.

## What the searches showed

1. **"Artists" means musicians for country queries.** "Famous South African artists" and
   "Ghanaian artists" return singer lists (briefly.co.za, yen.com.gh). Country hubs should also
   say "painters", "visual artists" or "photographers".
2. **Weak results on big topics.** "African masks meaning" and "African sculpture history" are
   full of spam PDFs and thin pages.
3. **Lists win.** "10 African artists you must know", "most influential…" titles rank everywhere.

## momaa.org in Semrush (30 October 2026)

- US: 5.8K keywords (−15% in a month), 7.1K visits/month (−9%), 79% informational intent.
  Worldwide: 13.4K keywords, 17.2K visits, Authority Score 36, 2K referring domains.
- **Most of its traffic is off topic.** Top page: "the Mona Lisa is worth $0" (18% of traffic),
  then bricolage, office design, calculators (framing, colour wheel, art insurance), even
  "8.8 kg to lb". Not worth copying.
- **On-topic wins worth copying:**
  - **Museum directory.** One page per African museum, mostly in the top 5: national museum Lagos (#1),
    Liberian national museum (#1), Zambia/Lusaka national museum (#1), national museum of
    Ethiopia (#2–5, 480/month), Benin City national museum (#1), house of slaves museum (#4,
    1.9K/month), Luxor museum, Olduvai museum, national museum of Tanzania, national museum of
    Mali. Each query is small (70–480/month, a few higher) but easy (KD mostly 14–50), and the
    pages earned links from Wikipedia (Lagos, Marrakech, Bronze and African art articles; nofollow)
    and afar.com. Its fifth page by traffic is the Ethiopia museum entry.
  - **Women artists list.** "50 most influential female artists": second page by traffic (9%),
    #4 for "famous female artists" (2.9K) and "famous female painters" (3.6K).
  - **Art forms and country art.** "african printmaking" (#1, 1K/month), "agbada" (#5, 2.9K),
    "asante stool" (#2, 590), "what are block prints" (#1), "eritrean art" (#1),
    "equatorial guinea art" (#1), "mali african art".
  - **Timeline tool.** "art movements" (#12, 8.1K), "art movements timeline", "chronology of
    art movements" — one interactive page.
- **Backlinks:** the top anchor, "tg @bhs_links - best seo li…", comes from 130 domains, and the
  top referring domains (artmiamimagazine.com, savannahherald.com, poptropi.ca, blogarama.com)
  look like bought or spam links. Do not copy.

## Ideas, in order

1. **African museums**: one page per major museum, in the languages each museum's visitors use.
   *Started 31 October 2026*: a `museums` collection (`/museums`) with ten pages — Lagos, Benin
   City, Accra, Addis Ababa, Bamako, the Théodore Monod Museum and the Museum of Black
   Civilisations in Dakar, Zeitz MOCAA, Rabat's MMVI and Cairo's Museum of Modern Egyptian Art —
   linked from the where-to-see guide, the art-form pages and the artists they hold. Next
   candidates, from MoMAA's rankings: Lusaka, Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Liberia, the House of Slaves
   on Gorée, Iziko and the Johannesburg Art Gallery, MACAAL, the Mokhtar Museum, Luxor, Gabon.
   Confirm volumes in Semrush first.
2. **Women artists**: check `guides/african-women-artists` targets "famous African female
   artists / painters"; consider a ranked-list format.
3. **Country hubs**: add "painters / visual artists" wording to titles and intros.
4. **New art-form pages**: African printmaking (we already have printmaking hubs), Asante stool
   and regalia; country-art pages where we have few artists.
5. **Masks and sculpture**: strengthen the existing pages; the competition is weak.

## Semrush data — 31 October 2026 (US database unless noted)

Pulled through the Semrush connector, about 7,500 API units.

### Our site

- 89 ranking keywords, almost all at positions 40–96 (normal for a site launched in October 2026).
  Best: "hand woven kente cloth" #13, "farouk hosny" #22, "amazwi contemporary art" #27,
  "ethio artist" #32, "mohamed mrabet" #32, "south african photographer kevin carter" #37.
- **Duplicate host:** 43 keywords rank on `africanartists.com` and 46 on `www.africanartists.com`
  (Benin Bronzes, kente, adinkra, mudcloth, Seydou Keïta… under www). Google has indexed both
  hosts, which splits their signals. Fix in Vercel → Domains: `africanartists.com` primary,
  `www` redirecting (308) to it; then check that `https://www.africanartists.com/art-forms/kente-cloth`
  lands on `https://africanartists.com/art-forms/kente-cloth`.
