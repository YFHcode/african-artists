# national-museum-of-mali (Bamako, Koulouba road at foot of Point G hill)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Founded 14 Feb 1953 as Musée soudanais by Georges Szumowski (IFAN Soudan section); became Musée national du Mali after 1960 independence; difficult period 1957–1982 (relocations, lack of means): afrik.com ; musicinafrica https://www.musicinafrica.net/node/3529 ; memoireonline
- Extension 2003 (Archnet 2002) curved forms inspired by traditional architecture; three permanent exhibitions: archaeology, ritual arts, textiles (AKDN 2007 release https://www.akdn.org/press-release/aga-khan-trust-culture-and-national-museum-mali-sign-collaboration-agreement ; Archnet https://archnet.org/sites/5252)
- AKTC agreement June 2006; AKTC helped build conservatory for archaeological and textile collections; National Park of Mali (103 ha) next door inaugurated Sept 2010, Francis Kéré gates/buildings (AKDN)
- Collections ~7,000 objects (afrik); photo archive 40,000 b&w photos (musicinafrica — weaker)
- Hosts portions of the Bamako photography biennale (Rencontres de Bamako) and contemporary art shows (musicinafrica; contemporaryand place page)
- 2013 struggled; holds artefacts from Timbuktu and Gao (Al Jazeera 2013)
- 21 April 2026: "Repenser le Musée national du Mali" project launched with Spain (apanews https://fr.apanews.net/diplomacy/bamako-engage-la-modernisation-du-musee-national/)
- Prince Claus Award laureate (princeclausfund page) — check year
- Rock shelter with paintings at Point G in grounds (afrik? memoireonline) — weak
- Not used (unverified): a street address, a bogolan collection, the 1982 building date, the museum's director.
