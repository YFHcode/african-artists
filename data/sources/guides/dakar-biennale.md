# dakar-biennale (Dak'Art, the Dakar Biennale; guide, batch 10, November 2026)

Sources for the English page; the French edition adds nothing. Facts as of 1 November 2026; the organiser had not published hours, admission or the full programme (re-check biennaledakar.org on 10 Nov and at the opening, 19 Nov).

- 16th edition, 19 Nov – 19 Dec 2026, Dakar; artistic director Morad Montazami (organiser https://biennaledakar.org/ ; allAfrica 6 Oct 2026 https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610060328.html ; allAfrica 24 Jul 2026 https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202607240217.html ; au-senegal.com https://www.au-senegal.com/dak-art-2026-tout-ce-qu-il-faut-savoir-sur-la-prochaine-biennale-de-dakar,18544.html )
- Theme « (Anti)fragilité : Arts de la réparation et stratégies du contrecoup » (au-senegal.com ; Africans Column https://africanscolumn.com/why-you-cant-miss-dakart-2026-dakar-biennale-sets-the-stage-for-its-most-anticipated-edition-yet/ ). The English rendering on the page is ours ("roughly"); no official English title found.
- Venues for 2026: Ancien Palais de Justice (Cap Manuel), Musée des civilisations noires, Galerie nationale d'art (organiser site). 2026 launch at MCN (existing museum source log).
- Former Palais de Justice: 1950s courthouse (one review gives 1958 inauguration, closed to court business 1992); hosted the international exhibition in 2016 and 2024 (Seneplus on 2024, https://seneplus.com/societe/dakar-la-decouverte-de-lart ; allAfrica Nov 2024 https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202411060376.html ). The page says only "of the 1950s" and "in recent editions, including 2024".
- 15th edition 7 Nov – 7 Dec 2024, curator Salimata Diop, title "The Wake / L'Éveil" (press; allAfrica Nov 2024 https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202411070132.html ; some pages mislabel this edition as 2026).
- MCN hosted most 2024 exhibitions (existing museum source log, allAfrica).
- History: 1990; visual art from 1992; contemporary African art from 1996 (existing source log of the fairs guide). Senghor, 1966 World Festival of Negro Arts, announced the museum (existing MCN log).
- Not used, unconfirmed: "54" artists (organiser site shows "54" beside "Exposition internationale" without a label); "five lines of flight" (one summary of the September communiqué); an honoured country (none found). Aggregator listing May–June 2026 contradicts the organiser: ignored.
