- Camerapix launched in 1963 from a small shop in Dar es Salaam, moved to Nairobi three years later; CEO of Camerapix Publishers International at his death: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Amin (pointer) ; https://artsandculture.google.com/story/inside-the-mohamed-amin-archive-mohamed-amin-foundation/kAWBxrRdULUo8g?hl=en ; https://www.deseret.com/1996/11/25/19279002/crash-killed-famine-photographer-aide/
