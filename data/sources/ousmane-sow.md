# Ousmane Sow

Expanded for batch 4, October 2026. Facts added or corrected by the expansion:

- Physiotherapist for close to three decades while sculpting on the side: https://momaa.org/icons-african-art/ousmane-sow/ ; https://www.livreshebdo.fr/article/le-sculpteur-ousmane-sow-est-mort
- Revealed in 1987 at the Centre Culturel Français, Dakar, with the Nuba series (one source gives a 1966 bas-relief at the Festival of Black Arts; not used): https://www.academiedesbeauxarts.fr/ousmane-sow
- documenta IX, Kassel, 1992 (some sources say 1993; 1992 matches documenta IX); Palazzo Grassi during the 1995 Biennale (the search did not support a 2000 Venice Biennale): https://momaa.org/icons-african-art/ousmane-sow/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ousmane_Sow (pointer)
- Toussaint Louverture sculpture, seven feet, National Museum of African Art, 2011: https://www.gallery.ca/node/3635 (listing) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ousmane_Sow (pointer, recheck)
- "Sculpteur des hommes debout" as a Senegalese press label (headlines only, not quoted as his words): https://www.seneplus.com/node/91995 ; https://au-senegal.com/ousmane-sow-ce-sculpteur-des-hommes-debout,11032.html
- Pont des Arts 1999: three million visitors "reported"; a retrospective of 68 sculptures per one source: https://www.masterworks.com/academy/artists/ousmane-sow ; https://africanscolumn.com/ousmane-sow-the-senegalese-master-sculptor-who-brought-african-figures-to-life/
- Académie des Beaux-Arts: foreign associate member in the seat of Andrew Wyeth; sources give 2012 (MOMAA, 11 April) or 2013; installed 11 December 2013 (Jeune Afrique): https://www.jeuneafrique.com/166793/culture/le-sculpteur-s-n-galais-ousmane-sow-entre-l-acad-mie-fran-aise/ ; https://www.academiedesbeauxarts.fr/ousmane-sow . Facts and summary still say 2012; the prose notes both dates.
- Not used: Fondation Cartier, a Dakar museum, bronze statues of Victor Hugo/Senghor (not found); the recipe of his material (not described in sources).

Where to see the work: no permanent display confirmed; the page says so.
