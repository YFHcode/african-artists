# Peter Magubane

Expanded for batch 7, October 2026.

- Born 18 January 1932, Vrededorp; grew up in Sophiatown; Drum 1954 as driver and messenger (one source) or 1955; darkroom assistant: https://sahistory.org.za/people/peter-sexford-magubane ; https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/president-pays-tribute-renowned-photographer-magubane
- 586 days in solitary confinement from 1969; banned for five years; Mandela's photographer from 1990; died 1 January 2024 aged 91: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Magubane (pointer) ; https://www.bostonglobe.com/2024/01/03/metro/peter-magubane-91-who-fought-apartheid-with-camera-dies
- Soweto 1976: persuaded protesters to let photographers work; first Black South African to win Best Press Photo of the Year (1958); Ramaphosa eulogy; NYT obituary: Boston Globe; SAnews
- Seventeen books, two banned (Black As I Am, 1978; Black Child, 1982); Unisa honorary doctorate 2003; Humanistic Award of the American National Professional Photographers Association; Order for Meritorious Service: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Magubane (pointer) ; https://unisa.ac.za/sites/corporate/default/Unisa-History-and-Memory-Project/Personalities/All-personalities/Peter-Magubane
- Science Museum Group record https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/cp159907 ; Northwestern visiting artist 2009 https://art.northwestern.edu/visiting-artists/peter-magubane-2009
- Not confirmed: a book titled Soweto; Order of Ikhamanga; the 1967 Rand Daily Mail, the 1971 arrest and 98 days (kept from the earlier page). "How to look" is the site's reading.
