# Malangatana Ngwenya

Expanded for batch 6, October 2026.

- Ball boy at a tennis club in Lourenço Marques; encouraged by Augusto Cabral and Pancho Guedes (credited with his move into full-time art in 1960); first public showing in a group show 1959, first solo show 1961: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malangatana_Ngwenya (pointer) ; https://momaa.org/icons-african-art/malangatana-ngwenya ; https://africanah.org/?p=32049
- Arrest by the PIDE for involvement with FRELIMO, dated 1964 in one source; imprisonment eighteen months in one source, about two years in another: Wikipedia (pointer); Africanah
- Poems in Black Orpheus and Modern Poetry from Africa (1963); Gulbenkian grant 1971 (printmaking and ceramics in Portugal); FRELIMO deputy 1990 until the first multiparty elections; died 5 January 2011, Matosinhos, Portugal: Wikipedia (pointer); https://manoeuvre.pt/en/artist/malangatana
- Tate artist page: https://tate.org.uk/art/artists/malangatana-ngwenya-18868 ; Richard Saltoun Gallery: https://www.richardsaltoun.com/artists/367-malangatana-ngwenya/
- Removed: nothing; "1965" arrest was never on the page. Murals' survival not confirmed. "How to look" is the site's reading.
