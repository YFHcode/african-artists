Sharon Dede Padi was born in Ghana in 1976. Working under the name Padiki, she developed a technique of printing with leaves — coating real leaves in paint and pressing them onto canvas — to build images of plants, figures and landscapes, celebrating nature and Ghanaian life. She was also a poet and spoke widely about creativity, women in the arts and the environment.

In Accra she made a leaf-print painting measuring 54.33 square metres, recognised by Guinness World Records as the largest of its kind. Shortly before her death she had been named visual arts ambassador for the 200th anniversary of the Odwira festival.

She died on 7 July 2026, aged 50; the news was met with tributes across Ghana's arts community. See our lists of [Ghanaian painters](/artists/country/ghana/painters) and [Ghanaian artists](/artists/country/ghana).
