Cameroon's grassfields kingdoms — Bamum, Bamileke and others — produced some of Africa's most spectacular court art: beaded thrones and figures, elephant masks, carved house posts and the brass work of Foumban. Modern Cameroonian art grew up largely outside formal schools, through workshops and art centres such as the one [Nzante Spee](/artists/nzante-spee) founded in Bamenda, and through studio photographers like Jacques Toussele, who photographed the people of Mbouda for decades.

Today Cameroonian artists are prominent worldwide. [Samuel Fosso](/artists/samuel-fosso) made his name with self-portraits taken in his Bangui studio; Barthélémy Toguo works across painting, sculpture and installation and founded the Bandjoun Station art centre; Pascale Marthine Tayou builds exuberant installations from everyday objects; Angèle Etoundi Essamba photographs African women. Joseph-Francis Sumégné, Goddy Leye and Hervé Yamguen are other names on this list of Cameroon's artists with Wikipedia articles, most looked-up first.
