Contemporary African art is one of the liveliest parts of the global art world, shown at the Venice Biennale, Documenta and every major museum, and collected across the continent and beyond (our guide to [contemporary African art](/guides/contemporary-african-art) tells the story). This list gathers the artists born in 1960 or later — the generation that grew up after independence, or in the diaspora — across painting, sculpture, photography and installation.

They include [Yinka Shonibare](/artists/yinka-shonibare), with his figures in Dutch wax cloth; [Wangechi Mutu](/artists/wangechi-mutu) and [Njideka Akunyili Crosby](/artists/njideka-akunyili-crosby); [Zanele Muholi](/artists/zanele-muholi), who photographs South Africa's Black queer communities; [Ibrahim Mahama](/artists/ibrahim-mahama), who wraps buildings in jute sacks; [Otobong Nkanga](/artists/otobong-nkanga), [Amoako Boafo](/artists/amoako-boafo), [Yto Barrada](/artists/yto-barrada) and [Ghada Amer](/artists/ghada-amer). Because the list is built from Wikipedia readership, it also includes photographers and others known mainly for other work, from the Bang-Bang Club's [Kevin Carter](/artists/kevin-carter) to the wildlife cameraman [Hamza Yassin](/artists/hamza-yassin). These are Africa's contemporary artists with Wikipedia articles, most looked-up first. See also [African painters](/artists/medium/painters) and [African photographers](/artists/medium/photographers).
