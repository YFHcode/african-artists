# museum-of-modern-egyptian-art (Cairo, Opera House grounds, Gezira/Zamalek)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Idea dates to 1927 (advisory committee / royal decree, initiative of Mohamed Mahmoud Khalil) (Arabic sources elwatannews; egypttoursplus — weak); moved several times; settled on Cairo Opera House grounds (Gezira exhibition grounds, Zamalek) in 1983 (rawi-publishing https://rawi-publishing.com/articles/museums-and-more?lang=en ; Ahram)
- 13,000+ works (Wikipedia; Scoop Empire); reopened Nov 2020 after renovation by Culture Minister Inas Abdel Dayem with new display (Youm7 14 Nov 2020 https://www.youm7.com/story/2020/11/14/... ; elwatannews https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/5070394 ; Kohllective visit report Nov 2021); ~890 works shown (Scoop Empire — attribute)
- Pioneers: Mahmoud Saïd, Mahmoud Mokhtar, Youssef Kamel, Mohamed Naghi (elwatan/aawsat); Saïd's La Ville in main hall (existing profile); Adam Henein works (existing profile)
- Free admission, upper floors closed (one visitor review — dated, don't use)
- Mokhtar Museum separate: pharaonic-temple style building by Ramses Wissa Wassef, 85 works (rawi)
- Reopened by minister Inas Abdel-Dayem, November 2020 (Youm7 announcement 14 Nov 2020; El Watan); Kohllective visit report says November 2020, Scoop Empire says December 2020 — November used.
