Roger Ballen was born in New York in 1950 and settled in South Africa in the 1970s, where for many years he worked as a geologist. Travelling through the small towns of the platteland, the rural interior, he began photographing the people he met there. The books *Dorps* (1986) and *Platteland* (1994) — square, frontal portraits of poor white South Africans in their homes — were admired and fiercely debated.

From the late 1990s Ballen's work became increasingly staged. In books such as *Outland* (2001) and *Shadow Chamber* (2005), people, animals, wires, stains and drawings on walls are arranged in cramped rooms into images that feel closer to dreams, or nightmares, than to documents. The style, which he calls "Ballenesque", reached a new audience when he directed the music video for Die Antwoord's "I Fink U Freeky" in 2012.
