Enriched for batch 9, October 2026 (kept short; not padded where sources ran out).

- Removed: "honorary Sudanese citizenship" (one weak source only) and "books long banned in Sudan" (no source).
- *Die Nuba* 1973, English *The Last of the Nuba* 1974; *Die Nuba von Kau* (*The People of Kau*) 1976, a different Nuba group. Sontag, "Fascinating Fascism", New York Review of Books, 6 February 1975; Riefenstahl answered in a memoir chapter.
- *Nuba Images* project, Staatliche Museen zu Berlin (nuba-images.smb.museum): Sudanese scholars and artists; about 10,000 photographs and films (dpa report); halted by the war from 15 April 2023.
