# Twins Seven-Seven

Expanded for batch 1 (pilot), October 2026. Facts added by the expansion:

- *Abiku*, spirit children believed to die and be reborn; *Healing of Abiku Children* (1973): https://guardian.ng/life/culture-lifestyle/abiku-a-thin-line-that-cuts-between-tradition-and-science/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Healing_of_Abiku_Children (pointer)
- Workshop participants given materials, space and encouragement but no academic instruction: `content/movements/oshogbo-school` (checked earlier)
- Ink and paint on plywood; low relief from a cut board mounted on a second board: https://indigoarts.com/artists/twins-seven-seven ; https://chazen.wisc.edu/new-accession-highlight-dogo-the-kidnapper
- Masonite, denim: https://studiomuseum.org/artworks/village-scene ; https://epackets.bowdoin.edu/artist-maker/info/5931
- *Esu-Odara's Giant Domestic Animals, Shade on the Wood* (1967): https://studiomuseum.org/artworks/esu-odaras-giant-domestic-animals-shade-on-the-wood
- *Dogo the Kidnapper* (1968–69), bought by the Chazen in honour of Prof. Emeritus Henry Drewal: https://arthistory.wisc.edu/faculty-news-artwork-purchased-in-honor-of-prof-emeritus-henry-drewal/
- *The Crucifixion* (1976), oil on plywood, gift of Dr Richard Long, High Museum 2006.106: https://high.org/collection/the-crucifixion/
- Chieftaincy title, Ekerin-Bashorun Atunluto of Ibadanland; *A Dreaming Life* (1999), edited by Ulli Beier from conversations mostly recorded in Sydney, December 1984 – February 1985 (Bayreuth African Studies 52): https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/004154579 ; https://muse.jhu.edu/article/208859/
- Died 16 June 2011 at University College Hospital, Ibadan, months after his mentor Ulli Beier: https://www.vanguardngr.com/?p=169915
