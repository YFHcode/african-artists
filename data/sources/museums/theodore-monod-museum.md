# ifan-museum-dakar (Musée Théodore Monod d'art africain, place Soweto, Dakar)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Building 1931–32 (au-senegal); institute IFAN founded 1936/1938 under Théodore Monod (founding director); museum first "Musée d'art africain de l'IFAN"; renamed Théodore Monod Dec 2007 (au-senegal https://au-senegal.com/musee-theodore-monod-d-art-africain-ex-ifan,1918.html ; blackpast)
- ~9,000–10,000 objects from 20+ countries, mostly West Africa: masks, figures, vessels, cloth (au-senegal; Goethe 2022 Dak'Art booklet https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf265/2022_dakar_livret_v6_dp.pdf ; presses du réel 9,727)
- Reopened May 2021 after renovation, "art back on African soil" (africanews https://www.africanews.com/2021/05/21/senegal-museum-reopening-celebrates-art-back-on-african-soil/)
- Dak'Art venue.
