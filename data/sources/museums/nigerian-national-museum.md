# national-museum-lagos (Nigerian National Museum, Onikan, Lagos Island)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Founded 1957 by English archaeologist Kenneth Murray (surveyor of antiquities); from 1946 Murray, with William Fagg (British Museum), bought Benin bronzes for a planned national museum; purchases ceased when museum opened 1957. Sources: Art Newspaper 2002 https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2002/04/01/how-the-queen-came-to-own-a-lagos-museum-piece ; Wikipedia Nigerian_National_Museum (pointer)
- Nok terracotta head from Jemaa (c. 900–200 BC) in collection: musicinafrica https://www.musicinafrica.net/node/4110 ; Google A&C "8 must-see masterpieces" https://artsandculture.google.com/story/kQVBsAXXcJSwJA
- Ife heads (bronze and terracotta) incl. Janus bronze head accessioned 1957: lagosmuseum.ng catalogue https://lagosmuseum.ng/handle/20.500.14596/2817 ; Google A&C
- Murtala Mohammed's car (assassinated 1976), bullet-riddled Mercedes, displayed: ikejarecord 2025 ; Google A&C
- ~300 items on display, 47,000 in store (Guardian Nigeria) https://guardian.ng/news/with-300-items-on-display-47000-in-store-national-museum-lagos-begs-for-attention/
- Ben Enwonwu's Anyanwu (original bronze, c. 1954–56, ~6 ft 10) stands outside, commissioned for the museum: Wikipedia Anyanwu; antique-collecting 2017 https://antique-collecting.co.uk/2017/02/16/world-record-for-african-artist-at-bonhams/ ; UN gifts replica https://www.un.org/ungifts/node/430
- 2025: Netherlands returned 119 Benin artefacts, handover at National Museum Lagos: arise.tv https://www.arise.tv/from-loss-to-legacy-at-the-official-handover-of-119-benin-bronzes-reclaimed-by-nigeria ; restitutionmatters https://restitutionmatters.org/news-item/7309
- Run by National Commission for Museums and Monuments (NCMM).
