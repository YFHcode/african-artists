Caleb Femi was born in 1990 in Nigeria and moved to London as a child, growing up on the North Peckham estate in south London. He trained and worked as an English teacher before becoming known as a poet, and in 2016 he was named London's first Young People's Laureate.

His first collection, *Poor* (2021), brings together poems and his own black-and-white photographs. It is a portrait of the estate and of the young Black men who grew up there — their friendships, their grief, the police and the concrete of the buildings that shaped them — and of how architecture and policy shape lives. The book was widely praised.

Femi also makes short films and works across poetry, photography and moving image. See our lists of [Nigerian photographers](/artists/country/nigeria/photographers) and [Nigerian artists](/artists/country/nigeria).
