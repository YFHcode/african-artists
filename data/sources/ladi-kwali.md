# Ladi Kwali

Expanded for batch 1 (pilot), October 2026. Facts added by the expansion:

- Coil-built without a wheel, standing and walking around the pot, walls beaten from inside with a flat wooden paddle; incised scorpions, lizards, crocodiles, chameleons, snakes, birds, fish: https://doodles.google/doodle/celebrating-ladi-kwali/ ; https://thepotterywheel.com/?p=6729 ; https://businessday.ng/news/article/10-things-to-know-about-ladi-kwali-first-woman-featured-on-naira/
- Cardew founded the centre in 1952; she joined in December 1954 as its first woman potter; Cardew arranged most of her exhibitions and demonstrations: https://awarewomenartists.com/en/artiste/ladi-kwali/ ; `content/art-forms/african-pottery` (checked earlier)
- Berkeley Galleries, London, 1958 (also 1959 and 1962 in some sources): https://awarewomenartists.com/en/artiste/ladi-kwali/ ; Bonhams Abuja pottery sale preview, February 2023 (Alamy caption)
- Fitzwilliam water jar, 1957, C.364-2016: hand-coiled stoneware, impressed and incised decoration, green and white slip, transparent glaze; an exhibition piece for European collectors, not functional: https://www.fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/work/water-jar ; https://fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/learning/look-think-do/water-jar
- Centre renamed after her; largely unused since the 1990s; December 2021 Niger State plan with the World Bank to rebuild it: https://dailytrust.com/the-shell-called-ladi-kwali-centre/ ; https://thecable.ng/womens-history-month-ladi-kwali-woman-who-put-nigerian-pottery-on-global-map
- Magdalene Odundo at Abuja in 1974: `content/artists/magdalene-odundo` (checked earlier); https://fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/conversations/podcasts/episode/magdalene-odundo-audio-memories-of-nigeria
- *Body Vessel Clay*, Two Temple Place, 29 January – 24 April 2022, curated by Jareh Das, anchored by Kwali with Odundo and younger UK-based artists; US debut at the Ford Foundation Gallery on the centenary of her birth: https://twotempleplace.org/exhibitions/body-vessel-clay/ ; https://www.craftscouncil.org.uk/whats-on/in-body-vessel-clay-black-women-ceramics-contemporary-art ; https://www.fordfoundation.org/about/the-ford-foundation-center-for-social-justice/ford-foundation-gallery/exhibitions/body-vessel-clay-black-women-ceramics-contemporary-art/
- Google Doodle, 16 March 2022: https://doodles.google/doodle/celebrating-ladi-kwali/
