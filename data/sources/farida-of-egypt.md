# Farida of Egypt

Enriched for batch 9, October 2026 (kept short; not padded where sources ran out).

- Born 1921, Alexandria; divorce 1948; Mathaf biography: began painting again after divorce, solo show at the Méridien hotel, Cairo, 1980, limited recognition; leaving Egypt 1963 and returning 1982 in one account.
- Mathaf holds *Niqash* (about 1950) and a pastel *Portrait of Simon Sfen*; also listed: Queen Farida Museum (Maadi), Alexandria Museum of Fine Arts, National Museum of Egyptian Civilization. Liwan Gallery show with granddaughter Yasmine Perreten. Died 16 October 1988 (Mathaf) or 17 October.
- Removed: Sufi inspiration, an Impressionist manner, exhibitions in the United States and the "abroad until 1974" claim (unconfirmed). "How to look" is the site's reading.
