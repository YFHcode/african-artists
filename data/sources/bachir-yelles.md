# Bachir Yellès

Enriched for batch 8, October 2026 (kept short).

- Born 12 September 1921, Tlemcen; died 16 August 2022, aged 100.
- Beaux-Arts Algiers 1943-47, Paris 1947-50 (teachers Narbonne and Untersteller). Directed the Algiers school 1960-80 in one biography; Radio Algérie calls him first director of the national school.
- Works named: *Portrait de Georges Marçais*, *Odalisque* (gouache, 1956). Pupil Boubekeur Sahraoui made 66 miniatures in homage.
- Not used: a museum holding and a 1962 date (unconfirmed). "How to look" is the site's reading.
