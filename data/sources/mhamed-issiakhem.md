# M'hamed Issiakhem

Expanded for batch 5, October 2026.

- Born 17 June 1928; died 1 December 1985, Algiers; accident of 1943 (grenade from a camp - French per Wikipedia, American per Africultures/AskArt; two sisters and a nephew killed; left arm/forearm amputated): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%27hamed_Issiakhem (pointer) ; https://africultures.com/personnes/?no=54573 ; https://www.barjeelartfoundation.org/artist/algeria/mohammed-issiakhem
- School of fine arts Algiers 1947-51 under Omar Racim; Paris 1953-58; returned 1962; cartoonist at Alger Républicain; founder of the National Union of Plastic Arts 1963: Wikipedia (pointer); https://www.artsy.net/artist/mhamed-issiakhem
- Met Kateb Yacine 1951; inseparable in Paris; introduced to theatre set design; friendship to 1985: https://www.algerie360.com/hommage-au-peintre-algerien-mhamed-issiakhem/ ; Africultures
- 1980: first Golden Lion of Rome (UNESCO) for African Art (Artsy), "Simba d'or" in the existing page; Femme et mur (1980): Artsy
- Banknotes and stamps: Numista lists a 5-dinar note; Wikidata: painter and postage stamp designer: https://en.numista.com/catalogue/artist.php?id=970 ; https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q3272339
- Musée national des Beaux-Arts d'Alger lists La Mendiante and Mère inconnue; 2010 catalogue by the national museum of modern and contemporary art, Algiers: Africultures
- Removed: "died of cancer" and "from 1965 he designed banknotes" (no source). The "how to look" paragraph is the site's reading of the sourced technique description.
