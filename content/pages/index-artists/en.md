Fourteen artists from twelve countries whose work changed how the world sees art from Africa — painters, sculptors and photographers, from the 1920s to today. Each profile covers the life, the work, the key dates and where the work can be seen.

The selection spans the continent and its languages on purpose: Anglophone West and East Africa, Francophone Mali, Senegal and the Congo, Lusophone Mozambique, Arabic-speaking Egypt, Sudan, Algeria and Morocco, and Southern Africa. For the schools these artists formed or rebelled against, see [art movements](/movements); for women artists beyond these profiles, see our guide to [African women artists](/guides/african-women-artists).
