## The oldest art

Southern Africa holds the oldest known drawing — a cross-hatched pattern on a stone flake from Blombos Cave, about 73,000 years old — and the painted slabs of Apollo 11 Cave in Namibia. The San and their ancestors painted and engraved rock shelters across the region for millennia, from the Drakensberg to the Tsodilo Hills and Twyfelfontein. See [African rock art](/art-forms/african-rock-art).

## Mapungubwe and Great Zimbabwe

On the Limpopo River, the kingdom of **Mapungubwe** flourished in the thirteenth century; its royal graves held gold objects, including a small gold rhinoceros that has become a national treasure of South Africa. **Great Zimbabwe**, which flourished from about the eleventh to the fifteenth centuries, built great dry-stone enclosures and carved soapstone birds that are now the emblem of Zimbabwe. Both are World Heritage Sites.

## Painting, beads and clay

Ndebele women in South Africa paint the walls of their homes with bold geometric designs and make beaded aprons, capes and blankets; [Esther Mahlangu](/artists/esther-mahlangu) took the tradition to Paris and onto a BMW. Zulu and Xhosa beadwork, and Zulu burnished black beer pots, are among the region's best-known arts. See [African beadwork](/art-forms/african-beadwork) and [African pottery](/art-forms/african-pottery).

## Modern art

In apartheid South Africa, art centres offered Black artists training they were denied elsewhere: the **Polly Street** centre in Johannesburg, from 1949, and the **Rorke's Drift** art and craft centre in KwaZulu-Natal, from the early 1960s, famous for printmaking and weaving; the Namibian printmaker John Muafangejo trained there. Gerard Sekoto (1913–1993), who painted township life and left for Paris in 1947, became a founding figure of Black South African modernism.

In Rhodesia, now Zimbabwe, a modern movement of [stone sculpture](/art-forms/shona-sculpture) grew from the late 1950s. In Mozambique, [Malangatana](/artists/malangatana) painted visionary canvases and murals, and in the north the [Makonde](/art-forms/makonde-art) carved masks and blackwood sculpture.

## Contemporary art

South Africa has one of the strongest contemporary art scenes on the continent, with Zeitz MOCAA and the Norval Foundation in Cape Town, the Johannesburg Art Gallery, and fairs in both cities. Its best-known artists include William Kentridge, known for animated charcoal drawings, the painter Marlene Dumas, and the photographer and visual activist Zanele Muholi. See [contemporary African art](/guides/contemporary-african-art) and [African photography](/art-forms/african-photography).

## Music

From township jazz and kwaito to [amapiano](/music/amapiano), the house music that has conquered dance floors across Africa and beyond, Southern Africa's music is as inventive as its art.
