# mohammed-vi-museum (MMVI, Rabat)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Inaugurated by King Mohammed VI Tue 7 Oct 2014; first museum in Morocco devoted entirely to modern & contemporary art; first major museum built since independence 1956 (CLAD); ~200m dirhams; ~10 years construction; renamed for the King Aug 2014 (le360); run by Fondation nationale des musées (FNM), president Mehdi Qotbi (lematin 2014 https://lematin.ma/journal/2014/le-souverain-inaugure-a-rabat-le-musee-mohammed-vi-d-art-moderne-et-contemporain_.../210151.html ; medias24 ; artnet https://news.artnet.com/art-world/moroccos-first-contemporary-art-museum-opens-128470)
- Inaugural show "1914–2014: 100 ans de création": ~400 works by 150+ Moroccan artists (Africanews 2024; fnm press pdf)
- Collection: ~500 works by ~200 Moroccan artists (2014 report) — dated
- Giacometti retrospective 18 Apr–4 Sep 2016 (medias24); "Face à Picasso" 2017, ~100 works from Musée national Picasso-Paris, first Picasso retrospective in Africa (ledesk; telquel); Goya/Cartier-Bresson (Africanews 2024)
- 10th anniversary 2024: 35+/38 major exhibitions (Africanews; cronica)
- 2026 programme 100% Moroccan (TelQuel 25 Feb 2026); Melehi retrospective "Œuvre en héritage" 29 Apr–31 Aug 2026 (FNM press release https://fnm.ma/uploads/press-releases/communiquedepresseexpositionmohamedmelehi-69fe219ab9382.pdf ; hespress ; telquel 30 Apr 2026)
- Chaïbia/CoBrA Jan 2025; Hassan El Glaoui "Sel de ma Terre" 2 Apr–31 Aug 2019 (existing profiles)
- 2020 group show: Cherkaoui, Gharbaoui, Belkahia, Kacimi, Melehi (fnh.ma)
- Architect: Karim Chakor (CLAD only — do not use)
