# Dan Eldon

Expanded and promoted to a full profile for batch 1 (pilot), October 2026. Facts added by the
expansion:

- Student Transport Aid: 1989 scouting drive Nairobi–Malawi; founded while at Pasadena City College; 1990 road trip through Tanzania to a refugee camp in Malawi; a vehicle given to Save the Children, money for wells and blankets for a children's hospital (counts of wells differ, so none given): https://www.cnn.com/WORLD/9712/07/eldon/index.html ; https://www.daneldon.org/through-the-years/dan-eldon-safari-news/ ; https://www.daneldon.org/dan-eldon-biography/
- One of the youngest photographers to work for Reuters: https://www.lensculture.com/books/5609-the-journey-is-the-destination-the-journals-of-dan-eldon ; https://www.npr.org/1998/01/09/1036865/dan-eldons-journals
- 12 July 1993: aerial attack on Aidid's command post, more than 70 Somalis killed; survivors urged journalists to come; Eldon, Hos Maina, Anthony Macharia (Reuters, soundman) and Hansi Krauss (AP) killed: https://cpj.org/data/people/dan-eldon ; https://www.refworld.org/reference/annualreport/cpj/1994/en/81165 ; https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/47534-kenyan-journalists-who-died-line-duty
- Seventeen journals, from his early teens; Chronicle Books, 1997, edited by Kathy Eldon; nearly 30,000 copies sold by late 1997: https://publishersweekly.com/pw/print/19970825/25070-pw-dead-fotog-22-built-own-epitaph.html ; https://www.lensculture.com/books/5609-the-journey-is-the-destination-the-journals-of-dan-eldon
- Kathy Eldon: published to inspire others to see their lives as works of art: https://www.npr.org/1998/01/09/1036865/dan-eldons-journals
- Creative Visions Foundation, founded by Kathy and Amy Eldon: https://edition.cnn.com/2017/11/23/us/dan-eldon-journey-is-the-destination ; https://huckmag.com/article/dan-eldon-and-the-power-of-creative-activism
- Film: Toronto International Film Festival 2016, directed by Bronwen Hughes, Ben Schnetzer and Maria Bello; Hughes came to the story through the book: https://2016.tiffr.com/shows/the-journey-is-the-destination ; https://filmsandfestivals.britishcouncil.org/projects/the-journey-is-the-destination ; https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/the-journey-is-the-destination-seeks-to-inspire-1.1896963

Where to see the work: the book (Chronicle Books, 1997, and later editions); daneldon.org.
