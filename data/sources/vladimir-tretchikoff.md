# Vladimir Tretchikoff

Expanded for batch 1 (pilot), October 2026. Facts added by the expansion:

- Ship sunk by the Japanese; prisoner on Java; released, rest of the war on parole in Batavia; Leonora ("Lenka"), lover and model, *The Red Jacket*: https://www.straussart.co.za/auctions/lot/8-nov-2020/308 ; https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2013/10/27/in-love-and-war
- *Chinese Girl* sitter Monika Sing-Lee, her uncle's laundry in Cape Town (one source says Johannesburg); she never liked the green face; bought from the artist in 1954 by a teenage daughter of an American businessman: https://thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/art/the-decoder-the-green-lady-1.648263 ; https://messynessychic.com/2021/06/23/the-master-and-his-muse-who-outdid-the-mona-lisa ; https://iol.co.za/news/south-africa/2013-03-21-r13-8m-chinese-girl-is-coming-home/
- Galleries rejected him; department-store exhibitions; Harrods, 1961, about 205,000 visitors: https://mg.co.za/friday/2024-03-15-tretchikoff-cant-be-brushed-aside/ ; https://absolutart.co.za/masters/vladimir-tretchikoff ; https://www.masterworks.com/academy/artists/vladimir-tretchikoff
- Prints authorised in huge numbers, sold cheaply in department stores and stationery shops (a Strauss & Co specialist, quoted in the AFP report of the 2025 sale): https://www.ewn.co.za/2025/05/28/safricas-king-of-kitsch-tretchikoff-sells-for-new-world-record ; https://www.enca.com/lifestyle/king-kitsch-tretchikoff-sells-new-world-record
- *Lost Orchid* among the ten best-selling prints in the UK in the 1950s: https://www.bonhams.com/auction/30638/lot/77/vladimir-griegorovich-tretchikoff-south-african-1913-2006-lost-orchid-framed
- *Lady from the Orient* (1955), Valerie Howe, daughter of a Cape Town grocer; print a major seller in 1960s Britain; record R31,892,000 (about US$1.78 million), Strauss & Co, Johannesburg, May 2025: https://www.ewn.co.za/2025/05/28/safricas-king-of-kitsch-tretchikoff-sells-for-new-world-record ; https://iol.co.za/weekend-argus/news/2025-05-28-vladimir-tretchikoffs-lady-from-the-orient-sets-new-auction-record-at-r31-9-million/ ; https://citylifearts.co.za/russian-born-south-african-painter-vladimir-tretchikoff-sells-a-portrait-painting-for-r31-million-at-a-joburg-auction-a-new-world-record/
- *Zulu Maiden*: https://sahris.org.za/node/97480
- 2011 Iziko retrospective, 26 May – 25 September, first major retrospective; Iziko had never acquired a work; about 80 works traced by the Tretchikoff Foundation; curator: very few South Africans under 50 had seen an original: https://visi.co.za/tretchikoffs-work-returns-to-cape-town/ ; https://www.capetownmagazine.com/events/tretchikoff-exhibition-at-iziko-gallery/11_37_53271 ; https://mg.co.za/?p=157228
- *Pigeon's Luck* (1973); Boris Gorelik, *Incredible Tretchikoff* (2013): https://www.artbookspublishing.co.uk/incredible-tretchikoff/ ; https://historicalnovelsociety.org/reviews/incredible-tretchikoff-life-of-an-artist-and-adventurer

Corrected: the facts box named the 2013 *Chinese Girl* sale as his record; the 2025 sale of *Lady from the Orient* beat it.

Where to see the work: no public museum holding confirmed. *Chinese Girl* is displayed by Laurence Graff in the Cape winelands (from the earlier, checked version of this page).
