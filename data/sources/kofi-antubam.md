# Kofi Antubam

Enriched for batch 8, October 2026 (kept short).

- Born 1922 (one article says 1923); died 1964, aged 42; place of death not agreed.
- State artist 1957; chair of state used on 1 July 1960, held at the National Museum, Accra.
- Education at Adisadel College, Achimota and Goldsmiths College.
