Ten genres, from song forms more than a century old — four of them now recognised by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage of humanity — to the dance-floor sounds streamed by millions every day. Each article explains where the music comes from, how to recognise it, the instruments and the artists who defined it.

Start with the pair most often confused: [Afrobeat](/music/afrobeat), the 1970s band music of Fela Kuti, and [Afrobeats](/music/afrobeats), the 21st-century pop that grew out of Nigeria and Ghana.
