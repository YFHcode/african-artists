# national-museum-of-ghana (2 Barnes Road, Accra)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Opened 5 March 1957 by the Duchess of Kent (Princess Marina) as part of independence celebrations: gmmb.gov.gh https://gmmb.gov.gh/?p=1044 ; musicinafrica directory
- Run by Ghana Museums and Monuments Board; largest and oldest of its six museums (?) ; >10,000 objects; archaeology, ethnography, fine art; Asante ancestral stools; sculpture garden with Nkrumah statue: Time Out Accra https://www.timeout.com/accra/museums/national-museum-of-ghana ; mainlymuseums
- Closed 2015 for repairs, reopened 2022 after ~7 years; President Akufo-Addo inaugurated refurbished gallery (Graphic) https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/president-akufo-addo-inaugurates-refurbished-museum-gallery.html ; GMMB "Bounces Back" https://gmmb.gov.gh/?p=1345 ; This is Africa review "superb restoration but painful stories untold"
- Building: architectuul (architect? check)
- Ghana building: Drake & Lasdun (Fry Drew Drake & Lasdun office), 1957; Lasdun's dome, prefabricated in UK and shipped (RIBApix https://www.architecture.com/image-library/RIBApix/licensed-image/poster/national-museum-accra-ghana-plan-and-section-showing-the-diagrammatic-analysis-of-building-climate-a/posterid/RIBA88007.html ; Wikipedia: with Ghanaian Public Works Department)
- Reopened Friday 10 June 2022 by President Akufo-Addo after closure since 2015 (Ghana Travel https://ghana.travel/2022/06/13/president-akufo-addo-re-opens-renovated-national-museum-to-boost-tourism/ ; Citi Newsroom 12 June 2022)
