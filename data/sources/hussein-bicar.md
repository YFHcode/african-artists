# Hussein Bicar

Expanded for batch 6, October 2026.

- Born 2 January 1913, Alexandria (1912 in two sources); Higher School of Fine Arts, Cairo, 1928; teachers Youssef Kamel and Ahmed Sabry; graduated 1933 (some say 1934); died 16 November 2002: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussein_Bicar (pointer) ; https://almasargallery.com/hussein-bicar/ ; https://dafbeirut.org/en/hussein-bicar
- Akhbar el-Yom: from 1945 (Almasar) vs full-time 1959-1962 (DAF Beirut); Sindbad launched January 1952, key cartoonist: Almasar
- Poet, musician, art critic; never learned musical notation; peasant women pulling water in terracotta jars; Qena and Nubia: https://www.masterworks.com/academy/artists/hussein-bicar ; https://cleg.art/?p=75649
- Teaching: drawing in Morocco from 1939 and Faculty of Arts in Qena (one source), Cairo fine arts academy (another): Wikipedia (pointer)
- Jordan National Gallery of Fine Arts holds a work: Masterworks. No Bicar museum found.
