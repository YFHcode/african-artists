summary: Misan Harriman (born 1977) is a Nigerian-born British photographer and filmmaker. Self-taught, he became widely known in 2020 for his photographs of Black Lives Matter protests in London, and that September became the first Black man to photograph the cover of British Vogue in its more than a century of publication.
---
Misan Harriman was born in Lagos in 1977 and moved to Britain as a child. He came to photography relatively late and taught himself, working first as an entrepreneur and only later picking up a camera seriously.

In the summer of 2020, after the murder of George Floyd in the United States, he photographed the Black Lives Matter protests in London. His pictures, shared widely online, captured both the anger and the tenderness of the marches. Weeks later *British Vogue* commissioned him to shoot its September 2020 cover, "Activism Now", making him the first Black man to photograph a cover of the magazine since it was founded in 1916.

He has since photographed public figures and campaigns, directed films and spoken widely about representation in the image industry. See our lists of [Nigerian photographers](/artists/country/nigeria/photographers) and [African photographers](/artists/medium/photographers).
