# zeitz-mocaa (Silo District, V&A Waterfront, Cape Town)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Opened 22 Sept 2017; 1920s grain silo (tallest building in Cape Town then), disused since 1990; Heatherwick Studio carved galleries; 9,500 m2 over nine floors; 80 galleries, 6,000 m2 exhibition space; R500m; private partnership V&A Waterfront + Jochen Zeitz; Zeitz collection part of permanent collection (zeitzmocaa.museum opening release https://zeitzmocaa.museum/zeitz-mocaa-opens-designed-by-heatherwick-studio/ ; heatherwick.com https://heatherwick.com/projects/buildings/zeitz-mocaa/)
- Mark Coetzee (founding director) suspended/resigned May 2018; Koyo Kouoh exec director & chief curator from 2019 (Art Newspaper, ARTnews); Kouoh died 10 May 2025 aged 57 (Boston Globe; Artforum); Elvira Dyangani Ose appointed artistic director Aug 2026, from October (Art Newspaper 2026 https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2026/08/13/elvira-dyangani-ose-appointed-artistic-director-of-cape-towns-zeitz-mocaa)
- Exhibitions: El Anatsui in opening show "All Things Being Equal" and "TSIATSIA – searching for connection" in atrium (elanatsui.art https://elanatsui.art/news/zeitz-mocaa-opens-in-cape-town); William Kentridge 2019 survey "Why Should I Hesitate: Putting Drawings to Work" in two parts (check title) ; Tracey Rose "Shooting Down Babylon" 18 Feb–28 Jun 2022 (Ammodo)
- 42 tubes, 27 m atrium from a scanned grain of corn, pillowed glass in the grading tower (now the Silo Hotel): Dezeen 2017 https://www.dezeen.com/2017/09/15/thomas-heatherwick-zeitz-mocaa-cape-town-art-museum-south-africa/ ; New Atlas ; The B1M
- Kentridge: Why Should I Hesitate: Putting Drawings to Work, from 25 Aug 2019, Norval Foundation sculpture half: zeitzmocaa.museum https://zeitzmocaa.museum/why-should-i-hesitate-putting-drawings-to-work-zeitz-mocaa-hosts-william-kentridge-survey/
- Muholi: Somnyama Ngonyama lunchtime tours Oct/Nov 2017, Feb 2018 (zeitzmocaa.museum); book launch 1 Dec 2018 (inkanyiso.org).
- Tracey Rose, Shooting Down Babylon, 18 Feb–28 Jun 2022, works 1996–2019: Ammodo https://www.ammodo.org/en/agenda/tracey-rose-shooting-down-babylon/
- The Art Newspaper comment 'Africa's private Tate Modern must do more for its public', 31 Oct 2018.
