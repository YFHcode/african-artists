# William Kentridge

Expanded for batch 2, October 2026. Facts added by the expansion:

- Johannesburg, 2nd Greatest City after Paris (1989): 16mm film transferred to digital video, about 8 minutes; Art Gallery of Western Australia holds it: https://collection.artgallery.wa.gov.au/objects/19270/johannesburg-2nd-greatest-city-after-paris-1-of-9-films-from-drawings-for-projection-series
- The Refusal of Time (2012): commissioned by dOCUMENTA (13), Kassel; five-channel video installation, steel megaphones, breathing machine inspired by the factory machine in Dickens's Hard Times; Philip Miller, Peter Galison (historian of science); jointly acquired by the Met and SFMOMA; Met accession 2013.250: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/499717 ; https://www.sfmoma.org/press/release/sfmoma-and-metropolitan-museum-jointly-acquire-ma/ ; https://d13.documenta.de/research/research/view/the-refusal-of-time-2012
- The Head & the Load (2018), Tate Modern Turbine Hall, with 14-18 NOW; African porters in the First World War; music, dance, film projection, shadow play: https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/performance/head-load ; https://www.praemiumimperiale.org/en/laureate-en/laureates-en/kentridge-en
- Triumphs and Laments (April 2016): images made by washing the biological patina from the Tiber embankment through stencils; the dirt recollects and the frieze dissolves: https://hyperallergic.com/william-kentridge-plans-massive-vanishing-mural-in-rome ; https://art.lincolncenter.org/blogs/news/46251713-william-kentridge-along-the-tiber-river-in-rome ; https://www.artforum.com/columns/william-kentridge-discusses-his-project-along-the-banks-of-the-tiber-river-228779/ (length stays "more than 500 metres": sources give 500 and 550)
- Not used: whether Tate holds Felix in Exile (no collection record found); the claim that the Rome frieze has already faded (only vandalism was reported).

Where to see the work: Met collection page for The Refusal of Time; AGWA collection page for Johannesburg, 2nd Greatest City after Paris.
