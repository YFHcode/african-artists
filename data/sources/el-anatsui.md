# El Anatsui

Expanded for batch 2, October 2026. Facts added by the expansion:

- Man's Cloth and Woman's Cloth (2001-02; sources give 2001 or 2002, the page says "around 2002"): bought for the British Museum with Art Fund help; the first works to use liquor bottle tops instead of wood; tops reclaimed from liquor bottling in Ghana and Nigeria: https://www.artfund.org/supporting-museums/art-weve-helped-buy/artwork/8741/mans-cloth ; https://www.britannica.com/biography/El-Anatsui ; https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/el-anatsui-scottish-mission-book-depot-keta/
- Old Man's Cloth (2003), aluminium and copper wire, Harn Museum of Art, purchased 2005: https://harn.emuseum.com/objects/10025/old-mans-cloth
- Gravity and Grace: Monumental Works by El Anatsui, Brooklyn Museum, 8 Feb - 18 Aug 2013, first solo show at a New York museum; organised by the Akron Art Museum: https://brooklynmuseum.org/exhibitions/el_anatsui/ ; https://copper.org/consumers/arts/2013/march/Brooklyn_Museum.php ; https://news.artnet.com/art-world/el-anatsuis-gravity-and-grace-exhibition-30953
- Behind the Red Moon (Tate Modern, Oct 2023 - 14 Apr 2024): eighth Hyundai Commission; three acts; first act The Red Moon, sail-like, recalls Atlantic ships; largest indoor work: https://tate.org.uk/press/press-releases/hyundai-commission-el-anatsui-behind-the-red-moon ; https://frieze.com/article/el-anatsui-turbine-hall-2023 ; https://artlyst.com/el-anatsui-sublime-bottle-cap-curtains-in-three-acts-fill-tates-turbine-hall/
- Process description (flattened, twisted, joined with copper wire): copper.org page above.
- Not used: Met holdings (no object or accession found); the Nsukka distillery as the source of the caps (the search did not confirm Nsukka); that his works are "regularly on view" (removed from the page).

Where to see the work: British Museum (Man's Cloth, Woman's Cloth); Harn Museum of Art (Old Man's Cloth); check displays before travelling.
