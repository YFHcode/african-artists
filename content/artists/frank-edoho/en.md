Frank Edoho was born in 1972 in Nigeria. He became one of the country's best-known faces as the host of *Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?*, the Nigerian edition of the international quiz show, which he presented from 2004 until 2017; he returned to host the show when it was revived in 2022, and later co-hosted *The Price Is Right*.

Most people who look him up are looking for the presenter. But Edoho is also a photographer, as well as a filmmaker, producer and voice-over artist, and photography is part of his creative work alongside television. That is why he appears among Nigeria's photographers in the data this site draws on.
