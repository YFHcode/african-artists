# Zanele Muholi

Expanded for batch 2, October 2026. Facts added or corrected by the expansion:

- Co-founded the Forum for the Empowerment of Women (FEW) in 2002 (Gauteng Black lesbian organisation); Inkanyiso founded in 2009 (page changed from "co-founded" to "founded", per SAHO and Britannica): https://sahistory.org.za/people/zanele-muholi ; https://www.Britannica.Com/biography/Zanele-Muholi ; https://thealit.de/zanele_muholi
- First solo exhibition 2004 (Market Photo Workshop course 2003 per one source); MFA in Documentary Media, Ryerson University, 2009, thesis on Black lesbian identity and politics in post-apartheid South Africa: https://thealit.de/zanele_muholi ; https://www.stevenson.info/artist/zanele-muholi/biography
- Corrected materials in Somnyama Ngonyama: scouring pads, latex gloves, rubber tyres (clothes pegs and "plastic" removed: not found in sources); safety pins, goggles and cable ties appear in some versions (not added): https://www.seattleartmuseum.org/whats-on/exhibitions/zanele-muholi ; https://www.spelman.edu/museum-of-fine-art/art-and-events/exhibitions/past-exhibitions/zanele-muholi-somnyama-ngonyama-hail-the-dark-lioness.html
- Somnyama Ngonyama touring exhibition organised by Autograph, London, shown with the Seattle Art Museum; Seattle's version counts 76 portraits, mainly 2014-2017, each with an isiZulu title; Muholi's first language is isiZulu, lives in Johannesburg: https://www.seattleartmuseum.org/whats-on/exhibitions/zanele-muholi ; https://fastforward.photography/zanele-muholis-exhibition-at-autograph-abp/
- Faces and Phases: black-and-white silver gelatin prints, large format, more than 240 portraits in one count; captions give name, place, year (e.g. Nosi 'Ginga' Marumo, Johannesburg, 2007, 76.5 x 50.5 cm): https://www.lensculture.com/books/9365-faces-and-phases-zanele-muholi ; https://stevenson.info/publication/zanele-muholi/faces-and-phases ; https://ncartmuseum.org/exhibition/zanele-muholi-faces-and-phases/
- Tate Modern survey, 5 November 2020 - 31 May 2021 (listing): https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/exhibition/zanele-muholi ; https://loeildelaphotographie.com/en/tate-modern-zanele-muholi/
- Represented by Stevenson (Cape Town/Johannesburg) and Yancey Richardson (New York): https://www.stevenson.info/artist/zanele-muholi/biography
- Not used: 600+ Faces and Phases portraits (single non-Tate source); Tate collection holdings (gallery text only); 2024-25 Tate return (unclear); documenta 13 and Brooklyn Museum 2019 (not found).

Where to see the work: Tate Modern (exhibition history); Stevenson and Yancey Richardson for current shows; Seattle Art Museum and other venues of the Autograph tour.
