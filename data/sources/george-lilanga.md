# George Lilanga

Enriched for batch 6, October 2026; stays a short profile.

- Said he was born 1934 in Kikwetu village, Masasi district (another source: 1935); died June 2005, Dar es Salaam; hired as a watchman at Nyumba ya Sanaa, then drew and painted (co-founding in 1973 unconfirmed); Tingatinga influence, animal paintings from about 1980: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lilanga (pointer) ; https://africanah.org/?p=4400 ; https://www.magnin-a.com/en/artists/68-george-lilanga/
- Washington group show: 1978 (280 works, 100 of them his) in one source; another says first New York trip 1978 and Washington show the next year: Wikipedia (pointer); Africanah
- Africa Remix: 137 works, 88 artists, 25 countries, initiated by Jean-Hubert Martin: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africa_Remix ; https://www.onart.media/en/large-format/05-key-exhibitions-for-the-recognition-of-contemporary-african-art/
- Not used: Keith Haring link, Pompidou and Guggenheim Bilbao shows (dealer claim), any Magiciens link. "How to look" is the site's reading.
