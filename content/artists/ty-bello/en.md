Toyin Sokefun-Bello, known as TY Bello, was born in 1978 in Ogun State, Nigeria, and studied economics at the University of Lagos. She worked briefly in journalism before turning to photography, and became a member of the Depth of Field collective of Nigerian photographers.

As a portrait photographer she has photographed leading Nigerians, including three sitting presidents, and served as official photographer to President Goodluck Jonathan. Her portraits and fashion photographs are known for their warmth and light, and she holds an annual exhibition to raise money for orphans through her charity, Link-a-Child.

Music runs alongside her photography. She first came to attention in the gospel group Kush before going solo with songs such as "Greenland" and albums including *Greenland* (2008).
