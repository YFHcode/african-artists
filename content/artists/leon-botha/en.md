Leon Botha was born in Cape Town on 4 June 1985 with progeria, a condition that ages the body many times faster than normal; most children with it do not live beyond their early teens. Botha lived to 26, longer than almost anyone else with the disease at the time, and was determined to live it fully.

He painted and drew, worked as a hip-hop DJ under the name DJ Solarize, and was part of Cape Town's music and art scenes. His first solo exhibition, *Liquid Sword; I Am HipHop*, opened in January 2007. In 2010 he and the photographer Gordon Clark made *Who Am I?… Transgressions*, an exhibition of portraits exploring his condition, shown at the João Ferreira Gallery in Cape Town. The same year he appeared in the music video for Die Antwoord's "Enter the Ninja", which brought him to a worldwide audience.

He died on 5 June 2011, a day after his 26th birthday. An opera inspired by him, *Solarize*, by the Polish composer Marcin Stańczyk, premiered in Warsaw in 2014. See our list of [South African artists](/artists/country/south-africa).
