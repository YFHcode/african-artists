Morocco's visual traditions are old and rich: Amazigh carpets and silver jewellery, painted wooden ceilings, zellige tilework and calligraphy. In the twentieth century these became the raw material of a modern art. In 1962 [Farid Belkahia](/artists/farid-belkahia) took over the art school in Casablanca, and with Mohamed Melehi, Mohamed Chabâa and [Mohamed Hamidi](/artists/mohamed-hamidi) built the [Casablanca School](/movements/casablanca-school). Others went their own way: the self-taught [Chaïbia Talal](/artists/chaibia-talal), Ahmed Cherkaoui with his signs, and [Hassan El Glaoui](/artists/hassan-el-glaoui), painter of horsemen.

Today Moroccan artists show around the world: the painter and novelist [Mahi Binebine](/artists/mahi-binebine), [Yto Barrada](/artists/yto-barrada), who represents France at the 2026 Venice Biennale, and photographers such as [Leila Alaoui](/artists/leila-alaoui) and [Hassan Hajjaj](/artists/hassan-hajjaj). Some well-known names here are better known for something else, like the novelist [Tahar Ben Jelloun](/artists/tahar-ben-jelloun) or the politician [Mahjoubi Aherdane](/artists/mahjoubi-aherdane), both lifelong painters. This list brings together Morocco's artists with Wikipedia articles, most looked-up first. See also [Moroccan photographers](/artists/country/morocco/photographers) and [North Africa](/regions/north-africa).
