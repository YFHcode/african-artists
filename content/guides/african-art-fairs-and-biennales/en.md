## Biennales and fairs: the difference

A **biennale** is a large curated exhibition, usually every two years, organised by a city or foundation. Works are shown, not sold, and the choices of the curators shape reputations. A **fair** is a commercial event where galleries rent booths and sell. Both matter, in different ways.

Dates move from year to year, and editions are sometimes postponed. Always check the official website before travelling.

## Biennales

| Event | City | Since | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dak'Art** — Biennale of Contemporary African Art | Dakar, Senegal | 1990 (visual art from 1992; dedicated to contemporary African art from 1996) | The oldest and best-known biennale devoted to contemporary art from Africa and its diaspora |
| **Rencontres de Bamako** — African Biennale of Photography | Bamako, Mali | 1994 | The leading platform for photography and video from Africa; associated with the rise of [Malick Sidibé](/artists/malick-sidibe) and [Seydou Keïta](/artists/seydou-keita) |
| **Lagos Biennial** | Lagos, Nigeria | 2017 | An independent biennial using the city's spaces |
| **Cairo Biennale** | Cairo, Egypt | 1984 | A long-running international biennale |

Dak'Art grew out of Senegal's long investment in the arts, described in our article on the [École de Dakar](/movements/ecole-de-dakar).

## Art fairs

| Fair | Where | Since | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1-54 Contemporary African Art Fair** | London, New York, Marrakech | 2013 (London) | Founded by Touria El Glaoui; the name refers to the 54 countries of Africa. London edition in October during Frieze week, New York in May, Marrakech in February |
| **Investec Cape Town Art Fair** | Cape Town, South Africa | 2013 | Now one of the largest art fairs on the continent; usually held in February |
| **FNB Art Joburg** | Johannesburg, South Africa | 2008 | The longest-running fair of its kind in Africa; usually in September |
| **ART X Lagos** | Lagos, Nigeria | 2016 | Founded by Tokini Peterside-Schwebig; the leading fair in West Africa, usually in November |
| **AKAA (Also Known As Africa)** | Paris, France | 2016 | A fair of contemporary art and design focused on Africa |

## How to make the most of a visit

- **Fairs**: go on a preview or opening day if you can; the best works sell first. Ask galleries for price lists — they will share them — and for the artist's exhibition history.
- **Biennales**: plan two or three days. The main exhibition is only part of it: Dak'Art and the Rencontres de Bamako are surrounded by independent "off" exhibitions across the city.
- **Talk to people**: artists, curators and gallerists attend all of these events and are usually happy to talk.
