# Mohamed Hamidi

Enriched for batch 8, October 2026 (kept short).

- Born 7 August 1941, Casablanca; died 6 October 2025, aged 84.
- Casablanca fine arts to 1958; Paris from 1959 (Grande Chaumière; ENSBA 1962; assistant to Jean Aujame; diploma in monumental art 1964); taught 1967-75; open-air show in Jemaa el-Fna 1969; AMAP co-founder, 1972 in one source.
- Centre Pompidou acquired *Harmonie* (1971) and *Marie* (1972) in 2019.
- Sources: Dafbeirut, Ocula, Loft Art Gallery. "How to look" is the site's reading.
