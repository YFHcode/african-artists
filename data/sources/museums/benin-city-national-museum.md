# benin-city-national-museum (King's Square / Ring Road, Benin City)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Opened 1973 under NCMM; ground and first-floor galleries opened Aug 1973, second gallery Aug 1996; on the Ring Road roundabout (King's Square). Collection began as antiquities in the palace under Oba Akenzua II, later moved to a room in the old tax office: Wikipedia Benin_City_National_Museum (pointer); guardian.ng https://guardian.ng/art/revue/a-voyage-around-benin-home-of-arts-culture/ ; dailytrust ; afktravel
- Holds Benin bronzes, terracotta, cast iron. Upgrade work reported 2020s.
- Restitution: Germany transferred ownership of 1,117 (or ~1,130) bronzes 2022; Smithsonian, MFA Boston returned pieces 2022; March 2023 presidential notice vested ownership of looted artefacts in the Oba Ewuare II; NCMM–palace management agreement; Cambridge transferred legal ownership of 116 to NCMM Feb 2026 (Museums Association https://www.museumsassociation.org/museums-journal/news/2026/02/cambridge-university-transfers-ownership-of-116-benin-bronzes-to-nigeria/); artnet https://news.artnet.com/art-world/benin-bronze-oba-ownership-2291586
- Igun Street guild (Igun Eronmwon) still casting by lost wax, most families on the street: Barnaby Phillips excerpt, Artnet https://news.artnet.com/art-world/barnaby-philips-benin-1967703 ; Met Perspectives https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/bronze-casters-igun-street
- MOWAA first building opened Nov 2025; bronzes plan set aside: as on the Benin Bronzes page (sourced there).
- Not used (unverified): the museum building's shape, distance from the palace, ivories in the collection.
