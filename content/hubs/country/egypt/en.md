Egypt has one of the longest art histories on earth, from the pharaohs' temples and statues to Coptic textiles and the mosques and manuscripts of Islamic Cairo. Modern Egyptian art began in 1908, when a free School of Fine Arts opened in Cairo. Among its first students was [Mahmoud Mokhtar](/artists/mahmoud-mokhtar), whose granite *Egypt's Awakening* became a national symbol; with the painter [Mahmoud Saïd](/artists/mahmoud-said) he founded a modern art rooted in Egyptian life.

The next generations were just as varied: the surrealists of the Art and Freedom group, the militant painter [Inji Aflatoun](/artists/inji-aflatoun), the illustrator [Hussein Bicar](/artists/hussein-bicar), [Effat Nagy](/artists/effat-nagy) with her folk magic, the sculptor [Adam Henein](/artists/adam-henein), and the abstract painter and longtime culture minister [Farouk Hosny](/artists/farouk-hosny). Today [Ghada Amer](/artists/ghada-amer), [Wael Shawky](/artists/wael-shawky) and [Anna Boghiguian](/artists/anna-boghiguian) show worldwide. The list also includes a queen who painted, [Farida of Egypt](/artists/farida-of-egypt). These are Egypt's artists with Wikipedia articles, most looked-up first.
