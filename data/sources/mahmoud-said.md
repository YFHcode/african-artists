# Mahmoud Saïd

Expanded for batch 4, October 2026. Facts added or corrected by the expansion:

- Born 8 April 1897; died 8 April 1964 at the family home in Gianaclis, Alexandria (asthma attack): https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/the-kurator%2Fshare%2Fegyptian-modernist-artist-mahmoud-sad-1.1625736713730 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmoud_Sa%27id (pointer)
- Judge: assistant judge at the Mixed Courts 1922, first judge in Mansourah 1927, Alexandria 1937; resigned 1947 aged 50 (one German entry says he stayed on until the 1950s; disputed): https://momaa.org/icons-african-art/mahmoud-said/ ; https://www.artdubai.ae/blog/rewind-mahmoud-said-as-told-by-hashem-montasser/
- Corrected record: The Whirling Dervishes (1929), Christie's Dubai, October 2010, $2,546,500 against an estimate of $300,000-400,000, the most expensive Middle Eastern painting then; it passed Les Chadoufs ($2,434,500, Christie's Dubai, April 2010). Page facts and summary previously gave Les Chadoufs as the record: https://m-news.artron.net/20101103/n131556.html ; https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/christies-make-record-14m-in-dubai-sale-1.503138 ; https://www.huffpost.com/entry/egyptian-painter-mahmoud_n_1877280
- Les Chadoufs dated 1934 (page) or 1935 (one source): the page says both.
- Only Arab artist with three paintings over $1 million at auction (as of the source): https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/the-kurator%2Fshare%2Fegyptian-modernist-artist-mahmoud-sad-1.1625736713730
- Museum in the Gianaclis villa, one account says opened 1999 with the Museum of Modern Egyptian Art and the Seif and Adham Wanly Museum; La Ville in the main hall of the Museum of Modern Art, Cairo: https://www.artdubai.ae/blog/rewind-mahmoud-said-as-told-by-hashem-montasser/ ; https://www.jmartmanagement.com/post/aristocratic-art
- Mixed Courts: international courts for cases involving foreigners (general knowledge).
- The "let the light lead" paragraph is the site's reading, built on the page's own description.
- Not used: Lady with the Golden Earring / Girl with Earrings (not confirmed), the $2.5 million sale attributed to the wrong house (the National confirms Christie's Dubai).

Where to see the work: Mahmoud Saïd Museum, Alexandria; Museum of Modern Egyptian Art, Cairo.
