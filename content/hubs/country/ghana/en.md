Ghana's art draws on rich traditions: the royal arts of the Asante, with their golden regalia, carved stools and brass goldweights, the woven [kente cloth](/art-forms/kente-cloth) and the stamped [adinkra symbols](/art-forms/adinkra-symbols). At independence in 1957, artists such as Kofi Antubam, the state artist of Kwame Nkrumah, and Amon Kotei, who designed the coat of arms, gave the new nation its visual identity, and the art school at Kumasi trained generations of painters and sculptors.

Today Ghana is one of the liveliest centres of contemporary art. El Anatsui, born in Ghana, is among the most celebrated artists alive; Ibrahim Mahama, Amoako Boafo and Serge Attukwei Clottey have built international careers and new institutions at home. The Ga workshops of Paa Joe and others make figurative "fantasy coffins" in the shapes of fish, cars and cocoa pods. These are Ghana's artists with Wikipedia articles, most looked-up first.
