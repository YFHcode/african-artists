# Inji Aflatoun (Efflatoun)

Expanded for batch 6, October 2026.

- Born Cairo 1924, died 1989; studied with Margo Veillon; Girl and Monster (1942) and The Girl of Port Said (1957) at Mathaf: https://mathaf.org.qa/en/encyclopedia/artists-biographies/inji-efflatoun/ ; https://qrcode.mada.org.qa/mathaf/MAT.2013.16.43.php
- Arrested 19 June 1959; Abi Zaabal and Qanater; release 1962 (Al Jazeera) or 1963 (other sources); painted in prison, portraits then landscapes; later "white" period and the tree as symbol of freedom; social realism after Mexican muralism: https://www.aljazeera.com/video/al-jazeera-world/2024/9/10/painting-from-prison-egypts-iconic-activist-inji-efflatoun ; https://shop.artjameel.org/products/the-life-and-work-of-inji-efflatoun ; https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/113777.aspx
- Memoir (1993) Mudhakirat Inji Efflatoun: Min al-Tufula ila al-Sajn; English From Childhood to Prison in The Life and Work of Inji Efflatoun (Barjeel Art Foundation, Skira, 2025; editors Sultan Sooud Al Qassemi and Suheyla Takesh): https://www.skira-arte.com/products/the-life-and-work-of-inji-efflatoun
- Removed: "educated at the French Lycée" (not confirmed); fact "July 1963" (sources differ). "How to look" is the site's reading.
