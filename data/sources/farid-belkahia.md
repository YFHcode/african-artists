# Farid Belkahia

Expanded for batch 7, October 2026.

- Born 15 November 1934, Marrakech; died 25 September 2014, Marrakech; Paris 1954 or 1955 to 1959; Prague theatre institute (stage design) 1959-62; Brera Academy, Milan, 1965-66; director of the Casablanca school 1962-74; hammered copper early 1960s, leather and henna from the mid-1970s: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farid_Belkahia (pointer) ; https://www.latelier21.ma/en/artists/57-farid-belkahia/biography ; https://mathaf.org.qa/en/encyclopedia/artists-biographies/farid-belkahia/ ; https://tate.org.uk/art/artists/farid-belkahia-23376
- Team arrivals: Melehi and Maraini 1964, Flint 1965, Chabâa 1966 (Tate); one French catalogue says all in 1964; Maraini's art-history course; Flint's classes; Plastic Presence in Jemaa el-Fna and 18 November Square (no year; the earlier 1969 is unconfirmed): https://tate.org.uk/press/press-releases/the-casablanca-art-school ; https://ocula.com/magazine/spotlights/moroccos-iconic-new-wave-the-casablanca-art-school/
- Fondation Farid Belkahia (Marrakech); 2017 show on his school years; Mathaf 2018-19; Tate St Ives 2023 (first major museum exhibition of the Casablanca Art School): https://www.tate.org.uk/tate-etc/issue-58-summer-2023/into-the-light-casablanca-art-school-tate-britain
- Not used: a 2021 Centre Pompidou retrospective (one market source only). "How to look" is the site's reading.
