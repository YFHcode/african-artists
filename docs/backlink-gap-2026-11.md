### F. Links we can place ourselves (low weight, real traffic)

**Substack** (both competitors) — run the newsletter there or mirror it. **Pinterest** (13 links to
momaa) — boards for patterns, textiles, masks, symbols. **Medium** — excerpts with a link back to
the full page. **Linktree or lnk.bio** — for the social profiles. These count for little alone, but
they bring visitors and get the site found.

### G. Encyclopedias — earned, never placed by us

Wikipedia (16 links to CAA, 100 to momaa), Wikidata, Wikiquote and their copies (Wikiwand,
Grokipedia, which copies citations). Editors cite pages that are sourced, stable and neutral.
**We do not add our own links** — that breaks Wikipedia's rules and gets removed.

### Not prospects

Search and portal pages (Bing, Yahoo, AOL), scrapers and B2B directories (Alibaba, Accio, Prospeo,
Store Leads), site-value tools, and the finance, lottery and travel-booking sites in momaa's list,
which link to its off-topic pages.

## 5. Rules

- No paid links, link swaps, guest-post networks or private blog networks.
- No self-placed Wikipedia or Wikidata links.
- Every pitch offers something the reader would want: a sourced fact, a drawing, a data table,
  an interview, a teaching resource.
- Log every pitch and result (prospect, date, page offered, answer) so the same site is not
  pitched twice.
