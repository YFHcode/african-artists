# Bruno Catalano

Expanded and promoted to a full profile for batch 1 (pilot), October 2026. Facts added by the
expansion:

- Born 1960 in Khouribga (two magazines say Casablanca; most biographies say Khouribga); family to Marseille in 1970 (one source says 1975): https://aste.capitoliumart.it/en/artist/catalano-bruno-1960/xar-1496 ; https://www.metzemaekers.com/kunstenaar/catalano-bruno/609874 ; https://www.artsper.com/us/contemporary-artists/france/2635/bruno-catalano
- Trained as an electrician by his father; at twenty, work on ships of a shipping company, then the SNSM: https://www.brunocatalano.com/sculpture-bronze/bruno-catalano-bio.php?EN=1 ; https://fondazionesorgentegroup.com/en/art-collection/arts-of-900/emilie
- Modelling classes in Marseille; own workshop and kiln; clay, then bronze (dates differ: 1981 or the 1990s, so none given): https://www.brunocatalano.com/sculpture-bronze/bruno-catalano-bio.php?EN=1 ; https://www.galeries-bartoux.com/en/artists/bruno-catalano
- The suitcase as a container of memories that steadies the emigrant, "roots in motion"; travels left him feeling part of him was gone: https://www.routedmagazine.com/exploring-emigration-art ; https://saffamag.com/?p=1923 ; https://www.dailyartmagazine.com/sculptures-of-bruno-catalano
- Figures linked by a thin metal strip through the suitcase: https://www.ecured.cu/Les_Voyageurs
- About thirty *Voyageurs* around Venice during the 2019 Biennale: https://venezianews.it/en/?p=57799 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Travelers_(sculptures) (pointer)
- Galeries Bartoux, SoHo, New York, 18 May – 18 June 2024; Arcachon outdoor summer show of six monumental works: https://www.galeries-bartoux.com/en/the-viewing-rooms/bruno-catalano-travel-to-new-york ; https://www.galeries-bartoux.com/en/bruno-catalano-in-arcachon/
- *Le Voyageur* in the Musée subaquatique de Marseille, tenth and last artist, sixteen sculptures: https://madeinmarseille.net/actualite/marseille/121103-video-le-musee-subaquatique-de-marseille-immerge-sa-derniere-statue/
- *Le Grand Van Gogh*: painted bronze, about 4 m, UPTEN, 201 10 Ave SE, Calgary, since September 2020; young Van Gogh with a bag and a painting on his back; two more in the lobby: https://www.strategicgroup.ca/post/van-gogh-monumental-statue-arrives-in-calgary-at-upten ; https://www.avenuecalgary.com/city-life/neighbourhoods/great-pieces-of-public-art-in-calgary

Where to see the work: Calgary (above); Musée subaquatique de Marseille (above).
