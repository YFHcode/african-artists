## From Kano to Antwerp

Otobong Nkanga was born in Kano, in northern Nigeria, in 1974. She began studying art at Obafemi Awolowo University in Ile-Ife, continued at the École nationale supérieure des Beaux-Arts in Paris, and was a resident at the Rijksakademie in Amsterdam. She settled in Antwerp, where she lives and works.

## Land and what is taken from it

Nkanga's subject is the ground: soil, rock and the minerals in them — copper, mica, malachite — and the long chains of mining, trade and consumption that link a hole in one landscape to wealth in another. She works across forms: large woven tapestries mixing photography and drawing, installations with stones and raw materials, diagrams, and performances in which she handles and offers those materials.

For documenta 14 in 2017, in *Carved to Flow*, she set up a workshop that produced soap from oils and butters from Africa and the Mediterranean, and used the proceeds to support a foundation in Nigeria — an artwork that was also an economy. Her tapestries picture the body and the landscape as inseparable, woven together with the materials taken from the ground.

## Recognition

She received a Special Mention at the 2019 Venice Biennale and has had solo exhibitions at major museums across Europe and North America.
