# national-museum-of-ethiopia (Addis Ababa, near Addis Ababa University / Arat Kilo? check)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Grew from Institute of Archaeology, founded 1958 to support French archaeological missions in northern Ethiopia; idea of National Museum after Ethiopian Cultural Heritage Administration established 1976: Wikipedia; HMML repository https://hmml.org/collections/repositories/Ethiopia/national-museum-of-ethiopia/ (1958)
- Basement: palaeoanthropology, casts of Lucy (Australopithecus afarensis, ~3.2 Ma) and Selam (~3.3 Ma); originals in storage (Lonely Planet). Prague 2023 loan press release (National Museum Prague) https://publikace.nm.cz/...
- Upper floor: Ethiopian fine art incl. Afewerk Tekle's "African Heritage"; painting of Solomon and Sheba (Lonely Planet). Afewerk Tekle and Mamo Tessema helped found museum (wiki snippet — weak).
- 2022: President inaugurated new archaeological exhibition with French Embassy (africanews https://africanews.com/2022/10/04/ethiopias-president-inaugurates-new-archaeological-exhibition)
- Capital Ethiopia 2025: brutalist landmark https://capitalethiopia.com/2025/11/16/national-museum-of-ethiopia-a-brutalist-landmark-houses-the-nations-history/ ; Reporter: Finance Ministry nods to construction of new National Museum https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/40042/
- Run by Ethiopian Heritage Authority.
- Prague: Lucy and Selam shown 25 Aug–23 Oct 2025, Lucy's first showing in Europe, Selam's first abroad; Lucy toured US 2007–2013: Smithsonian Magazine https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/meeting-lucy-how-a-world-first-european-exhibition-brought-visitors-face-to-face-with-the-fossil-that-shrinks-time-180987690 ; Africanews 27 Aug 2025
- Floors (ground: pre-Aksumite to Gondarine, bronze lamp, rock-hewn chair, Haile Selassie throne; first floor: art from possibly 14th-c. parchment to 20th-c. canvases, Afewerk Tekle's African Heritage; second floor: crafts; basement: Lucy casts): Lonely Planet https://www.lonelyplanet.com/ethiopia/addis-ababa/attractions/national-museum/a/poi-sig/455957/355289 . MOMAA's directory places the royal regalia on the first floor; Lonely Planet followed.
- Location between Arat Kilo and the AAU graduate school; Ethnological Museum at Sidist Kilo in Haile Selassie's former palace: city administration landmarks page; Lonely Planet.
