# Khadija Saye

Reviewed for promotion in batch 4, October 2026 and promoted to a full profile (688 words in English; hand-written facts in five languages; linked from three articles).

- Parents both from The Gambia; mother Mary Mendy (Christian), father Muslim and surviving; University for the Creative Arts, Farnham; mentored by Nicola Green, co-founder of the Diaspora Pavilion (2016); youngest participant: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khadija_Saye (pointer) ; https://www.photomonitor.co.uk/dwelling-space-breathe-works-khadija-saye-venice-biennale-2017/ ; https://www.frieze.com/article/khadija-sayes-reflections-spirituality-african-diaspora
- Series of nine tintypes, six shown in Venice (Wikipedia says a series of six); objects from spiritual healers in The Gambia by exchange and prayer; made from a need for spiritual grounding after trauma; Toor-Toor = "flower" (Wolof); tééré amulet and cow horn; Nak Bejjen, 250 x 200 mm: https://sayephoto.format.com/dwelling ; https://www.victoria-miro.com/news/1313 ; https://prm.ox.ac.uk/node/2166761 ; https://africanah.org/khadija-saye
