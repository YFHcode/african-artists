# Malick Sidibé

Expanded for batch 5, October 2026.

- Born Soloba (about 300 km from Bamako), 1935 or 1936 depending on source; studio year 1958, 1960 or 1962; Bagadadji; died 14 April 2016: https://www.britannica.com/print/article/1370803 ; https://collezionemaramotti.org/documents/20182/20664/biosidibe-eng_pe10_cont/9f85532e-fc51-4441-9fcb-fbf82f330d2d ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malick_Sidib%C3%A9 (pointer)
- Hasselblad Award 2003, first African; citation on atmosphere and vitality of an African capital: https://www.hasselbladfoundation.org/malick-sidibe-2/ ; Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement 2007, first photographer and first African: Britannica
- Mali Twist, Fondation Cartier, 20 October 2017 to 25 February 2018 (some sources say 28 February), André Magnin and Brigitte Ollier, more than 250 photographs, title echoes a 1963 Boubacar Traoré song; "reporter of the youth"; Nuit de Noël "instant snatched from time": https://www.fondationcartier.com/en/programme/exhibition/malick-sidibe-mali-twist ; https://domusweb.it/en/art/2017/11/03/malick-sidibe-and-the-sweet-life-in-bamako.html ; https://thisisafrica.me/arts-and-culture/mali-twist-largest-exhibition-malick/
- The Met holds Les Frères et Soeurs (1967); notes dancing to records from the Beatles to cha-cha-cha: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/787814 ; Tang Teaching Museum (Skidmore): https://tang.skidmore.edu/collection/artworks/1408-des-chaussures-pour-aller-danser
