# museum-of-black-civilisations (Musée des civilisations noires, MCN, Dakar, opposite Grand Théâtre)

Museum page, batch 1, October 2026 (docs/competitor-research-2026-10.md). Sources for the English page; the translations add nothing.

- Inaugurated 6 Dec 2018 by President Macky Sall; idea announced by Senghor at 1966 World Festival of Black/Negro Arts; China-funded (~US$30–35m); ~14,000 m2, four storeys, capacity ~18,000 works; Hamady Bocoum director at opening (artnet https://news.artnet.com/art-world/museum-of-black-civilizations-1409911 ; africanews https://www.africanews.com/2018/12/07/senegal-unveils-museum-of-black-civilisations/ ; smithsonianmag)
- Design: circular, inspired by Casamance impluvium houses (and Great Zimbabwe per some) (hsozkult review https://www.hsozkult.de/exhibitionreview/id/reex-130868 ; lepetitjournal ; le360 location opposite Grand Théâtre)
- Sabre attributed to El Hadj Omar Tall: on display since opening on loan; handed over by PM Édouard Philippe to Macky Sall Nov 2019; French law 2020 transferred ownership (Sénat pjl20-015) (le360 2019 ; senat.fr)
- "Demoon dikkaat – Les Restitués" exhibition for 6th anniversary (Dec 2024)
- Dak'Art 2024 (7 Nov–7 Dec): MCN hosted most exhibitions incl. guest countries; Dak'Art 2026 19 Nov–19 Dec 2026 launch at MCN, artistic director Morad Montazami (allafrica)
- "Picasso à Dakar 1972–2022" exhibition at MCN (2022)
- No permanent display; first exhibition 'Civilisations africaines : création continue de l'humanité'; Toumaï skull and ancient furnace at inauguration; Bocoum on iron 2,500 BC; Duval-Carrié's baobab: Hespress https://fr.hespress.com/34411-senegal-le-musee-des-civilisations-noires-voit-le-jour.html ; Le Temps https://www.letemps.ch/culture/hamady-bocoum-sommes-africains ; RTBF https://www.rtbf.be/article/de-toumai-a-l-anti-colonialisme-les-civilisations-noires-entrent-au-musee-a-dakar-10091609
- 'A cast of' the Toumaï skull is the site's reading: the original is in N'Djamena.
