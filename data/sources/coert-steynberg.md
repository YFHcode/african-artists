# Coert Steynberg

Enriched for batch 8, October 2026 (kept short).

- Born 7 January 1905 near the Hennops River, Pretoria; died 28 July 1982.
- Dias statue 1933, South Africa House; Kruger statue at the Kruger Gate; Pretorius monument, Graaff-Reinet; Peace of Vereeniging monument.
- Springbok designs 1946-47 for a five-shilling coin, later used on the Krugerrand (1967); South African Mint Natura series 2017.
- Removed: "later work moved towards abstraction" (unverified). "How to look" is the site's reading.
