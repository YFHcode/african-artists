# Chéri Chérin

Enriched for batch 8, October 2026 (kept short).

- Born Joseph Kinkonda, 16 February 1955, Léopoldville; died 19 October 2025. Ceramics at the Académie des Beaux-Arts; signs and murals in N'Djili.
- Weltmuseum Wien holds 1999 works (*Death of the State of Zaire*, *If I Was President ... or King of the Animals*, *Les réfugiés de Brazzaville*, *Catastrophe de Ndolo*).
- Group shows: *Congo Art Works* (Bozar, 2016-17), *Congo Stars* (Tübingen and Graz, 2018-19), *Congo Paintings* (Vichy, 2019); JP Mika homage 2019.
- Not used: Beauté Congo/Cartier 2015 and Landry Mulala (not confirmed). "How to look" is the site's reading.
