# Chaïbia Talal

Expanded for batch 3, October 2026. Facts added or corrected by the expansion:

- Discovery: in 1965 her son invited Pierre Gaudibert to their home; he came with Ahmed Cherkaoui and André Elbaz (the page said her son introduced her to Cherkaoui and Gaudibert): https://momaa.org/icons-african-art/chaibia-talal/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cha%C3%AFbia_Talal (pointer)
- Paris debut 1966, Galerie Solstice; Salon des Indépendants (page's earlier wording kept; one source says Surindépendants): https://www.masterworks.com/academy/artists/chaibia-talal ; https://mathaf.org.qa/en/encyclopedia/artists-biographies/chaibiya-talal/
- Exhibited in Frankfurt, Copenhagen, Ibiza, Menton, Rotterdam (page's earlier "Americas" removed: not found): https://www.masterworks.com/academy/artists/chaibia-talal
- Mixed reception in Morocco in the 1960s: mostly male leaders of artistic movements rejected her paintings: https://momaa.org/icons-african-art/chaibia-talal/ ; https://mwnlifestyle.com/2025/03/26/chaibia-talal-the-self-taught-moroccan-art-icon/
- Influence of CoBrA noted by MOMAA; Chaïbia/CoBrA: Au croisement des libertés, Mohammed VI Museum, January 2025, about 20 works, mostly from the permanent collection: https://fr.le360.ma/culture/le-mmvi-demarre-lannee-avec-une-exposition-croisee-de-chaibia-talal-et-du-mouvement-cobra_3AGAXE5FC5HJ5DJ2HSX6UNTHXM/ ; https://fr.hespress.com/405325-le-mmvi-abrite-une-rencontre-entre-chaibia-tallal-et-le-mouvement-cobra.html
- 2018 Rabat show criticised by her son Hossein Talal for lack of consultation: https://telquel.ma/2018/10/17/les-cris-dorfraie-du-monde-lart-contre-une-expo-au-musee-mohammed-vi_1614642
- Institutions: Institut du monde arabe, Mathaf, Mohammed VI Museum "have folded her into the record" (momaa; one auction-market page also lists Barjeel).
- Removed: "exhibited in the Arab world and the Americas"; "blue sails" dream kept as on the page (not re-checked).
- Death date left as 2004 (sources give 2 or 4 April).

Where to see the work: Mohammed VI Museum, Rabat; IMA, Paris; Mathaf, Doha (check displays).
