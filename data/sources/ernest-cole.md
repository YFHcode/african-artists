# Ernest Cole

Expanded for batch 3, October 2026. Facts added or corrected by the expansion:

- Left school in 1953 (Bantu Education), diploma by correspondence; Drum 1958 as assistant to Jürgen Schadeberg; freelance for Drum, Rand Daily Mail and Sunday Express from the early 1960s, South Africa's first Black freelance photographer: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Cole_(photographer) (pointer) ; https://www.magnumphotos.com/photographer/ernest-cole/ ; https://www.all-about-photo.com/photographers/photographer/1237/ernest-cole
- Reclassified as "coloured" at twenty (one account; phrased as such): https://www.lensculture.com/ernest-cole ; https://www.blind-magazine.com/news/ernest-cole-photographer-without-a-passport/
- Briefly associated with Magnum; funding from the Ford Foundation and Time-Life; House of Bondage published by Random House, 1967; banned in perpetuity 1968 and passport removed: https://www.magnumphotos.com/photographer/ernest-cole/ ; https://ascleiden.nl/content/library-weekly?page=6
- Aperture re-issued House of Bondage in 2022: stated in a search summary only; page keeps it (low risk; recheck): https://store.magnumphotos.com/collections/ernest-cole
- Sweden 1969-71, Tiofoto collective; from 1972 life in disarray, stopped working, lost control of his archive: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Cole_(photographer) (pointer) ; https://www.lensculture.com/ernest-cole
- Death: pancreatic cancer, New York, aged 49, a week after Mandela's release (18 Feb per the page; one source says 19 Feb): https://www.magnumphotos.com/photographer/ernest-cole/
- Negatives: more than 60,000, Stockholm bank vault, 2017; handed to heirs, Ernest Cole Family Trust, April 2018; 504 Hasselblad Foundation prints in dispute (2020), nearly 500 vintage prints handed to the trust in 2024: https://goodman-gallery.com/news/ernest-cole-anti-apartheid-photographer-gets-his-due ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Cole_(photographer) (pointer)
- Corrected: birthplace shown as "Pretoria" (sources give Eersterust; the page had Eastwood): https://www.magnumphotos.com/photographer/ernest-cole/ . Facts line changed in all editions.
- Removed: "had no fixed home in New York" (not confirmed).
- Tate Modern display Artist and Society includes Cole: https://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-modern/display/artist-and-society/ernest-cole
- Not used: the "Haitian director" and Cannes details (kept as on the page, not re-checked).

Where to see the work: Tate Modern (Artist and Society display; check if on view); Magnum Photos; the film Ernest Cole: Lost and Found.
