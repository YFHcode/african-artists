Boniface Mwangi was born in 1983 and grew up in Nairobi. He became a staff photographer for *The Standard*, one of Kenya's main newspapers.

After the disputed presidential election of December 2007, violence between supporters of rival candidates and ethnic communities killed more than a thousand people and displaced hundreds of thousands. Mwangi photographed it from the first days, and some of his images were too violent for newspapers to print. His work from those months won him the CNN MultiChoice African Photojournalist of the Year award, which he won twice.

Convinced that Kenyans needed to see what had happened so that it would not happen again, he created *Picha Mtaani* ("street photos"), a travelling exhibition shown in towns across the country. In 2012 he founded PAWA254, a space in Nairobi for artists, journalists and activists. He has since become one of Kenya's best-known activists, campaigning against corruption and police violence, and has stood for parliament.
