Sani Mohammed Maikatanga was born in 1975 in Kano, the largest city of northern Nigeria, and began taking photographs as a secondary school student in the early 1990s. In 2000 he started work as a reporter and photographer for *Fim*, a popular magazine about Kannywood, the Hausa-language film industry based in Kano, and later worked for the newspaper *Leadership* and for Media Trust, publisher of the *Daily Trust*.

His work documents the culture of northern Nigeria: festivals, horsemen and durbars, markets, crafts, landscapes and daily life. His best-known subject is the Argungu Fishing Festival in Kebbi State, where thousands of fishermen enter the river at once; his photograph *Fishers on the Run* won first prize in the Global Landscapes Forum Africa photo competition in 2022.

His images are widely shared on social media and have brought northern Nigerian traditions to a large audience. See our lists of [Nigerian photographers](/artists/country/nigeria/photographers) and [African photographers](/artists/medium/photographers).
